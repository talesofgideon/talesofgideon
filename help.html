Digital Product & Device Help

Quick Setup Guides & Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome! If you recently purchased a digital eBook, Audiobook, Bundle, or Musical Score from Tales of Gideon, this guide will walk you through downloading, extracting, and loading your files across your favorite mobile phones, tablets, e-readers, and computers.

Delivery emails from noreply@talesofgideon.com are sent automatically upon order completion. Your secure download link remains active for 72 hours. We recommend downloading and saving your files directly to your device or personal cloud storage right away.

1. EPUB eBook Setup Guide

All Thomas Gideon digital books are delivered in standard, unencrypted .EPUB format. The EPUB standard is universally supported by almost every modern reading app and e-reader device.

Apple Devices (iPhone, iPad, Mac)

  • Tap the download button in your delivery email using Safari.
  • Once downloaded, tap the file in your Safari Downloads list or open the Files app.
  • Tap the .epub file; your device will automatically open it in Apple Books and sync your reading position across all your Apple devices via iCloud.

Android Phones & Tablets

  • Tap the download button to save the .epub file to your device.
  • Open your favorite e-reader application, such as Google Play Books (tap Upload to Play Books), ReadEra, Lithium, or Moon+ Reader.
  • Select your downloaded file to begin reading with full font, margin, and night mode customization.

Amazon Kindle (e-Readers & Kindle Apps)

  • Amazon Kindle natively supports standard EPUB files via Amazon's official Send to Kindle service.
  • Visit amazon.com/sendtokindle on any browser, sign into your Amazon account, and drag-and-drop the .epub file.
  • Alternatively, email the .epub file to your personal @kindle.com delivery address.
  • Your book will appear in your Kindle Library within minutes with custom fonts, Whispersync, and dictionary support.

Kobo & Other Dedicated e-Readers

  • Connect your Kobo, Nook, or dedicated e-reader to your computer using a USB cable.
  • Drag and drop the downloaded .epub file into your e-reader's storage drive, or manage your library using the free open-source software Calibre.

Windows PC & Mac Desktop

  • Mac: Double-click the file to open instantly in the built-in Apple Books app.
  • Windows PC: Open your EPUB file using free readers such as Calibre, Thorium Reader, or browser extensions like EPUBReader.

2. Audiobook & Audio File Playback

Our audiobooks and musical album scores are provided in high-fidelity .MP3 audio files, designed for universal offline playback on all smartphones, smart speakers, media systems, and in-car dashboards.

iPhone & iPad

  • Save the audio file to your device's Files app.
  • To listen with playback speed control, sleep timers, and chapter bookmarks, we recommend free specialized audiobook apps such as BookPlayer or Bound from the App Store. Simply open the app and import the audio file directly from the Files app.

Android Devices

  • Save the audio file to your device storage.
  • Open using your preferred media player or dedicated audiobook apps like Smart AudioBook Player, Voice Audiobook Player, or Listen Audiobook Player for resume tracking and chapter bookmarking.

Mac & Windows PC

  • Double-click the audio file to play in Apple Music, QuickTime, Windows Media Player, or VLC Media Player.

3. Opening Bundles & .ZIP Archives

If you purchased a Bundle (EPUB eBook + Audiobook) or a full Musical Album, your download is delivered as a convenient .ZIP archive containing all format files.

  • iOS (iPhone / iPad): Open the Files app, locate the downloaded .zip file, and tap it once. iOS will automatically extract all files into a companion folder.
  • Android: Open your device's Files or Downloads app, tap the .zip file, and select Extract.
  • Mac: Double-click the .zip file to extract all items immediately.
  • Windows PC: Right-click the .zip file and choose Extract All....
